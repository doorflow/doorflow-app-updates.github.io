DoorFlow Desktop

Design, print and encode cards (i.e. ID and access control)

Everything you need to design, encode and print access and ID cards. Create professional cards in minutes from a range of templates, add your own logos and cardholder photos, then encode and print them whenever you need to.

DoorFlow Desktop works with the tools you already have. Sync cardholders automatically from Microsoft Active Directory, Google Workspace, Azure or LDAP — no more manual data entry — print straight to your existing card printer without the usual driver headaches, and encode contactless passes, barcodes and biometric data for secure identification and access. Design for free, and upgrade to unlock database integration, encoding and printing when you’re ready.

  • No manual entry — sync cardholders straight from Active Directory, LDAP and other platforms.
  • Print to your own hardware — compatible with most card printers, including Evolis, Magicard and Zebra.
  • Smart encoding — contactless identification, 1D/2D barcodes and biometric data, handled while you design.
